Spam Email & Message Detection using Deep Learning

How an LSTM recurrent neural network processes sequential context to isolate spam with high precision.

By Mohammed Ilyas · 24BCA1337 · III BCA · Yenepoya Institute of Arts, Science, Commerce and Management, Mangaluru

About the Project

The problem and why it matters

Email and SMS are prime targets for phishing links, financial scams, and malware distribution[cite: 1]. Conventional keyword matching and Naive Bayes bag-of-words fail when adversaries permute phrasing[cite: 1].

Deep sequence models like Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) preserve word ordering, syntax, and long-range dependencies, delivering resilient classification[cite: 1].
